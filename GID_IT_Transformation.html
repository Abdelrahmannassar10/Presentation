04 · Security

Zero ransomware and zero wiper incidents in four years.

Achieved by in-house design and daily discipline, with no outsourced SOC, no security consultancy and no advanced security-platform purchase. Baseline firewall and endpoint protection were in place. This counts incidents, not attempts.

صفر حوادث برمجيات فدية أو مسح بيانات خلال أربع سنوات، بتصميم وانضباط داخلي.

    Before 2023 · E-commerce

    Site attacked with fake orders

    Response: SSL, hosting and cPanel hardening, order-pricing exposure addressed in 2023.

    2025 Q3 · Web

    Security incident from the Puvana website

    Response: investigated with an impact report; the KSA site moved to a company server and mail ports were secured.

    Neither event progressed to ransomware or wiper malware. Exposure avoided is a risk reduction, never a cash saving.
